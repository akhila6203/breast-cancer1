WE’RE HERE TO HELP

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about breast screening, consultations and care.

When should I consider breast screening?

Screening recommendations depend on your age, personal history and risk factors. A clinician can help you choose the right schedule.

What happens during my first consultation?

Your specialist will discuss your concerns and medical history, explain any recommended examinations and answer your questions.

Does every breast lump mean cancer?

No. Many breast changes are non-cancerous, but any new lump or persistent change should be checked by a qualified healthcare professional.

Can I get a second opinion?

Yes. You can discuss your diagnosis and care plan with our team and ask for help understanding your options.

What support is available during treatment?

Support may include symptom management, counselling, rehabilitation and guidance for family members, depending on your needs.
